## Rule

Final pages are NOT blindly implemented. Each page must have:
- unique intent
- unique content
- unique value

No thin doorway pages. Service pages only cover services the client actually provides.

## Page grouping

| Group | Pages | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Pillar | `/christmas-decoration-dubai` | Commercial "Christmas Decoration Dubai" |
| Services | villa, home, office, corporate, lighting, outdoor | Service-level intent |
| Regional | `/christmas-decoration-uae` | UAE-wide search visibility |
| Trust | gallery, about, areas-we-serve, contact | Brand + conversion |
| Extended | other-occasions | Wider business range |

## Build order

1. Homepage (hero, intro, services, signature, gallery, CTA, footer)
2. Pillar page `/christmas-decoration-dubai`
3. Service pages
4. Supporting pages (gallery, about, areas, contact, other-occasions)
