# Off-Page SEO — Branded Searches, Engagement, Backlinks & Broken-Link Building

**Audience:** Festive Occasions (client) walking through what off-page work is,
what we can do from the site, and what actions the client owns.
**Honesty rule (same as on-page):** no bought links, no link farms, no fake
reviews or fake listings. Everything below is legitimate and durable.

## 1. Branded searches (own the name)

Goal: when people search "Festive Occasions" or "Festive Occasions Dubai",
they land on the real site — and see consistent, correct information.

**Already handled on the site:**
- Brand in every `<title>` (`… | Festive Occasions`).
- `Organization` schema with `name`, `legalName`, `sameAs` (Instagram) on
  every schema-bearing page.
- `WebSite` schema on the homepage.
- One verified Instagram handle displayed in the footer: `@festive_ocassions`.

**Client actions (recommended):**
1. **Claim the Google Business Profile** for "Festive Occasions" with the
   verified NAP (Business Centre, Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, Sharjah).
   This is the single biggest branded + local search lever.
2. Keep the Instagram handle/name identical everywhere (Instagram, GBP,
   website) — consistency builds trust signals for "Festive Occasions".
3. If ever a second handle or an "official" claim appears elsewhere, update the
   site footer/schema to match the verified one.

## 2. Increasing engagement signals

Engagement doesn't mean fake likes — it means real people interacting with
the brand, which Google reads as relevance.

**Add these routinely (client-owned, 10–15 min/week):**
- Post real installation photos to Instagram during the season; tag the
  location, add the phone/WhatsApp in the bio.
- Reply to every comment and DM; redirect conversations to WhatsApp for quotes.
- Ask (never buy) satisfied clients if they'd allow a photo with a caption —
  this feeds the site gallery with fresh, client-verified imagery.
- Share blog posts to Instagram Stories when a new guide publishes.

**Site-side (already done):**
- WhatsApp prefilled-CTA across the site (`wa.me/971564284444`).
- Verified Instagram link in the footer ("Follow").
- Real client photos in gallery + ImageGallery schema (client images surface in
  Google Images for keyword searches).

## 3. Backlinks (external links pointing at festiveoccasions.ae)

Only honest, earned backlinks. Google rewards relevance + authority, not volume.

**Priority order (highest impact first):**
1. **Google Business Profile** — the GBP itself links to the site from a
   high-trust Google property.
2. **Local directories (free, legitimate):** Google Maps/GBP, Bing Places,
   Apple Business Connect, Foursquare, Yelp for business. Use the exact same
   NAP each time.
3. **UAE business / trade directories** — one or two well-known local listings
   (e.g., local business registries with real moderation), same NAP.
4. **Guest content** — offer one well-written seasonal article (e.g., "How to
   plan Christmas decoration for a Dubai villa") to a UAE lifestyle magazine or
   blog that accepts contributor pieces, with one contextual link back.
5. **Partners and venues** — every restaurant, hotel or office the studio
   decorates can link the site from their own site/social ("Decorated by
   Festive Occasions"). This is the most natural link source for a service
   business.

**Never do:** paid link networks, blog-comment spam, PBNs, or "submit to 100
directories" services. They risk a manual penalty and are wasted money.

## 4. Broken-link building (free, effective)

Find dead pages on relevant sites, then offer your content as the replacement.

**How (client can do with a free tool, or delegate):**
1. Pick 5–10 relevant sites (UAE/Villa/interiors/events blogs, directories).
2. Use a free broken-link checker (e.g., the "Broken Link Checker" browser
   extension) on those sites.
3. Find a 404 page about Christmas decoration, festive styling, or parties.
4. Email the site owner: "Your page about X is broken — we have a detailed
   guide on Christmas decoration planning you could link instead."
5. Pitch the relevant site URL: `/blog/when-to-book-christmas-decoration-dubai`,
   `/blog/christmas-decoration-packages-dubai`, or `/christmas-decoration-dubai`.

**Site-side (already done):** every blog post is a linkable asset with
canonical URLs, and every page carries internal links to the core service
pages — so any earned link passes through the whole site.
