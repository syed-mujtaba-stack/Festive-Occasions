# Google Business Profile — Step-by-Step Setup (Client Guide)

**Audience:** Festive Occasions (client) — non-technical, screen-by-screen.
**Cost:** ₹0 / AED 0 — **Google Business Profile is completely FREE.** Google never
charges for listing a business. Any "setup fee" you are quoted is an agency
charging for *their* work — we can do this ourselves for free.
**Time:** ~15 minutes to create + 1–7 days for Google to verify/review.
**Why:** this is the listing that appears in Google Maps and the "Christmas
decorators near me" search result. Without it, those searches cannot find the
business on Google Maps at all.

> Optional: the business address can stay **completely hidden** (service-area
> mode). Only phone + website + service areas are shown. This is the standard
> setup for decorators who work at client properties. Choose the option that
> feels right — both are free.

## Step 1 — Open the sign-up page (1 min)

1. On your phone or computer, go to: **https://www.google.com/business**
2. Click **"Manage now"** (or "Start now").
3. Sign in with the Google account you want to own the listing.

## Step 2 — Add the business (2 min)

4. Type the business name: **Festive Occasions**
5. If it appears in the suggestions — click it (someone may have created a draft).
   If not, click **"Add your business"**.

## Step 3 — Choose the category (1 min)

6. Type a category and pick the closest real one, e.g. **"Event decorator"** or
   **"Decoration service"** (whatever exists; the point is to be accurate).
   - Category affects which searches the listing appears in.

## Step 4 — Location (2 min) — the address-privacy decision

7. Google asks: **"Do you serve customers at your business address?"**
   - **"No"** → service-area business → your office address is **never shown
     publicly**; you only list service areas. *Recommended for Festive
     Occasions if you prefer not to publish the free-zone office address.*
   - **"Yes"** → the office address appears on Google Maps (fine too, if you
     are comfortable — some clients like seeing a Maps pin).
8. If you chose "No": add service areas — start with **Dubai** and **Sharjah**,
   and add the Dubai communities you actually serve (e.g., Palm Jumeirah,
   Jumeirah, Dubai Marina, Downtown, Business Bay, Arabian Ranches — only the
   honest list; you can edit later).

## Step 5 — Contact details (1 min)

## Step 6 — Verification (2–5 min — the only "waiting" part)

11. Google shows verification options:
    - **Phone (SMS or call)** → enter the code you receive on +971 56 428 4444.
    - **Video verification** → Google may request a short video (point the

In the GBP dashboard (business.google.com or the "Google Business Profile"
app):

17. **Attributes** — mark what fits (e.g., "Appointments required", "Accepts
    new clients" if true).
18. **WhatsApp / enquiry** — some regions allow adding a WhatsApp number; the
    phone field already covers it (+971 56 428 4444 is your WhatsApp).

## Step 8 — Publish + reviews (ongoing, 5 min/week)

19. The listing is live once the profile shows "Published".
20. Get your **review link** from the dashboard:
    - On desktop: Profile → "Ask for reviews" → copy the short link.
    - On the app: Profile → "Ask for reviews" → share.
21. Send that link to satisfied clients (the site's own ask: *"If you're happy

22. **Reply to every review** — a 1-line thank you; Google notices activity.

## Rules that keep the listing safe

- **NEVER** buy or incentivise reviews — Google detects fake reviews and can
  remove the listing entirely. This is the one thing that can lose everything.
- Keep the info identical to the website (name, phone, address, hours) —
  consistency builds trust with Google.
- Don't add services/locations you don't genuinely provide/serve.
- Ignore any "pay to rank", "pay to verify", or "GBP is paid" offers — the
  listing is free and this guide is everything needed.

## What if verification is stuck?

- Common waits: 1–2 weeks for Google's review. That is normal — the profile is
  usually created instantly and becomes searchable on Google Maps immediately
  even while "Pending"; full verification only unlocks reviews/edits.
- If something is wrong after 2 weeks, reply on this ticket and the dev side
  will walk through the exact screen (screenshots welcome).
